# Surroundings

The four environment maps here are from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com),
licensed CC0 (public domain), downscaled to 512 × 256 from the 1k
originals: `apartment` is Lebombo, `studio` is Studio Small 03, `sunset`
is Venice Sunset and `night` is Dikhololo Night. They light and reflect
in the 3D room when View settings asks for them; the studio's own light
panels are the default and need no file.
